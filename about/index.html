About

A study site for one class.

unibook holds the lessons, presentations, articles and videos of the 10 modules in Semester 5, 3rd year English (Licence), at the Department of English, University Hassiba Benbouali of Chlef.

Who made it

Bennafla Ridha made it and keeps it up to date. Every lesson starts from a teacher’s own handout or slides, and the teacher’s name and references are printed on the lesson page.

The wording is simplified, the meaning is not changed. If a page and the handout ever disagree, the handout is right, and the page should be fixed. Tell Bennafla Ridha.

How to use it

  1. Pick a module on the home page, or search for a word from the lesson.
  2. Read the lesson one bite at a time. Each bite is one idea.
  3. Answer the quiz and flip the flashcards. They show you what you have not yet kept.
  4. Press “Mark as done” at the end. The module lists show it next time.
  5. Look for “Likely in the exam” near the end of a lesson. Those points are the ones to learn first.

What stays on your phone

There are no accounts, no tracking and no ads. Your quiz answers and the lessons you have read or marked as done are saved in this browser only. Clear the site data, or open the site on another phone, and they start again from zero.

The only outside request the site can make is a YouTube video, and only after you press play. It loads from youtube-nocookie.com.

Use with AI

Paste this link into ChatGPT or Claude

https://roboticchlef02-dev.github.io/llms-full.txt

That one file holds every published lesson as plain text, quizzes and flashcards included, so an assistant can answer from the real material. For a shorter map, give it llms.txt. Each lesson also has a Markdown copy: add .md to its address in place of the final slash.

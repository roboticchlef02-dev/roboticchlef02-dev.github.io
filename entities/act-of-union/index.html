Act of Union

Under the Act of Union, the formation of Great Britain with the unification of England and Wales with Scotland.

The handout names two Acts of Union. In 1707, “the formation of Great Britain with the unification of England and Wales with Scotland.” In 1801, “the formation of the United Kingdom (England, Wales, Scotland, and Ireland; later to be separated, and the establishment of Northern Ireland in 1921 leading to the formation of the United Kingdom of Great Britain and Northern Ireland).”

It adds: “Despite their union, each state has its own identity and defining features.”

Concept

Accent

The way a speaker pronounces a language. An accent concerns pronunciation only.

An accent is the way a speaker pronounces a language. It concerns pronunciation only: the sounds, the stress and the melody of speech. A dialectconceptDialectA variety of a language that differs in vocabulary and grammar as well as pronunciation, linked to a region or a social group.Read more goes further and also differs in vocabulary and grammar.

Everyone has an accent. It can show where a speaker comes from, which social group they belong to, or which language they spoke first.

Example from New York

William LabovpersonWilliam LabovAmerican linguist (1927 to 2024) and a pioneer of variationist sociolinguistics. He studied the dialects and accents of New York.Read more linked pronunciation to social class in New York. In department stores of different social levels, he asked shop assistants where a department was. The answer was “the fourth floor”, and he listened to how they pronounced the r. How often the r was pronounced differed from one store to another, which showed a link between a feature of accent and social class.

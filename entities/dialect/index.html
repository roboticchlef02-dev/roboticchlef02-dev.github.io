Concept

Dialect

A variety of a language that differs in vocabulary and grammar as well as pronunciation, linked to a region or a social group.

A dialect is a variety of a language. It differs from other varieties in its vocabulary and grammar as well as in its pronunciation. An accentconceptAccentThe way a speaker pronounces a language. An accent concerns pronunciation only.Read more concerns pronunciation only.

Point of differenceAccentDialect
PronunciationYesYes

Two kinds

  • A regional dialect goes with the place where people live.
  • A social dialect goes with a social group, for example a social class or an ethnic group.

Example

In Scotland, “bairn” is a word for a child. It is vocabulary, so it belongs to the Scottish dialect and not only to a Scottish accent.

Not a worse language

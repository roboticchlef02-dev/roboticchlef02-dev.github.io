Edwardian era

A British Civilization in Transition, and a period when British imperial power was evolving.

Section 3.1 of the syllabus summary is headed “Contextualizing the Edwardian Era: A British Civilization in Transition”.

Its first subsection, 3.1.1, is “Edwardian Britain as a Global Power: the evolution of British imperial power in the Edwardian era, which brings in the concept of interculturality.”
